Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CR Industries LLC ("CR Industries," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website, crindustries.xyz. It covers the website only. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked from the app and from its App Store listing.

1. Who we are

CR Industries LLC is an independent software company based in the United States. You can reach us any time at info@crindustries.xyz.

2. Information this website collects

This is a static informational website. We do not run analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies on it, and we do not ask you to create an account or submit forms here.

  • Server logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically record standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) to serve the site and protect against abuse. That processing is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Email you send us. If you email an address on this domain, we receive your message and email address so we can reply. We use it only to respond to you.

3. How we use information

We use the limited information above only to operate and secure the website and to respond to messages you send us. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Our apps

Data practices for our apps are described in each app's own privacy policy, not this one. For our package-tracking app, see the WhereIsIt Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party links

This site links to third-party websites (for example, our product sites and GitHub). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their own policies.

6. Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

7. Your choices and rights

Because this website does not collect personal information beyond what is described above, there is little to manage here. If you have emailed us and would like us to delete your message and contact details, email info@crindustries.xyz and we will do so. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; contact us to exercise them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and post the new version on this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@crindustries.xyz.
